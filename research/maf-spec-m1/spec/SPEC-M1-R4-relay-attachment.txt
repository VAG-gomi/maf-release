R4-001 (D-025) — BASELINE TRAINING PROCEDURES, EXACT:
  B-POOL: MLP 6->16(tanh)->1 linear, biases, PyTorch default init.
    Training: the R3-001 loop with environments 1..20 in ascending order,
    300 epochs (6000 steps), batch = all rows of the current environment
    (trial envs: 400 obs + 100 interventional), loss = mean Gaussian NLL
    over the batch (single head serves both row types), plus 1e-4 weight
    decay on all parameters. No psi/U terms exist. Adam lr 1e-3.
  B-ENVNN: identical loop, batch, loss, and weight decay as B-POOL.
    Differences, enumerated: (a) input is x_full CONCATENATED with a
    learned 4-dim environment embedding; (b) embedding table has 25 rows
    (one per environment id), init N(0, 0.01), trainable; (c) holdout
    envs use the ZERO embedding row (declared handicap, unchanged);
    (d) embedding participates in both observational and interventional
    rows identically (it has no do-awareness — that is the point).
  B-IRML: B-POOL's architecture and loop exactly; loss gains the
    invariance penalty: for each environment batch, add
    lambda_iv * (mean residual over that batch)^2, where residual is
    (y - prediction) and lambda_iv is the per-world selected value from
    {0.1, 1.0} — selection by trial-env interventional RMSE on the
    100-row interventional sets of envs 1..10, evaluated AFTER training
    both candidates; the better candidate's full fit is the recorded one.
    (Both candidates are trained; the selection is post-hoc per world,
    which is the only selection D originally permitted.)
  B-MIXED: unchanged from R3-003. No early stopping exists anywhere in
    SPEC-M1; fixed step counts only.
